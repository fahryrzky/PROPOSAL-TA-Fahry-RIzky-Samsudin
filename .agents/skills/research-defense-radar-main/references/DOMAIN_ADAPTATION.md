# Domain Adaptation

The default fingerprint and weights fit empirical economics, finance, management, and adjacent quantitative social science. Adapt explicitly rather than forcing every project into that rubric.

## Theory

Replace data, unit, and empirical identification with primitives, assumptions, equilibrium concept, mechanism, comparative statics, welfare/result, and proof technique. A competitor can be direct even with a different application if it establishes the same theorem or mechanism.

## Experiments

Emphasize intervention, randomization unit, treatment implementation, measurement, preregistration, sample, estimand, manipulation checks, and external validity. Distinguish conceptual replication from design replication.

## Computer science and machine learning

Emphasize task definition, benchmark/data, architecture/algorithm, training regime, evaluation protocol, baseline, compute, ablation, reproducibility, and claimed capability. Conference/open-review chronology may dominate journal dates.

## Biomedical and life sciences

Emphasize population/model organism, intervention/exposure, endpoint, assay, design, bias control, clinical relevance, preregistration/trial identifier, and correction/retraction status. Treat medical implications as high stakes and prefer primary registries and publisher records.

## Qualitative social science

Emphasize research question, cases/site, sampling, source materials, conceptual lens, coding/interpretive method, triangulation, reflexivity, and theoretical contribution. Do not force causal-identification scoring.

## Humanities

Emphasize corpus/object, archive, period, interpretive question, conceptual intervention, theoretical framework, method, and historiographic claim. Citation neighborhoods and book chapters may matter more than recent preprints.

## Adaptation rule

Document the chosen dimensions and weights before scoring. Keep contribution claim and core question prominent. Mark inapplicable fields `NA`; never treat them as absent overlap. If a credible numeric rubric would imply false precision, use a structured qualitative matrix instead.
