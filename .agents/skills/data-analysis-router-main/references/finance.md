# Finance Analysis

Use for revenue, cost, profit, expense, cash flow, budget, receivable, payable, or management accounting data.

## Common Tables

- Revenue: `date`, `business_unit`, `product`, `customer`, `revenue`.
- Cost: `date`, `cost_type`, `business_unit`, `amount`.
- Expense: `date`, `department`, `expense_category`, `amount`.
- Profit: `revenue`, `cost`, `gross_profit`, `net_profit`.
- Cash flow: `date`, `cash_in`, `cash_out`, `balance`.
- Budget: `period`, `department`, `budget`, `actual`.

## Core Metrics

- Revenue, cost, gross profit, gross margin.
- Operating expense, expense ratio.
- Net profit, net margin.
- Cash inflow, cash outflow, ending cash balance.
- Budget completion and budget variance.
- YoY, MoM, or period-over-period growth.

## Analysis Models

- Profit structure: decompose revenue, cost, margin, expense, and net profit.
- Cost and expense analysis: identify high-growth or high-share categories.
- Cash flow analysis: compare cash inflow/outflow and pressure periods.
- Break-even analysis: use fixed cost, variable cost, and unit economics when available.
- Budget execution: compare actual vs budget by department, category, and period.
- Variance analysis: separate volume, price, mix, cost, and timing factors when data supports it.

## Chart Suggestions

- Revenue/cost/profit trend line chart.
- Profit waterfall chart.
- Expense category share chart.
- Budget vs actual bar chart.
- Cash flow trend and balance chart.
- Variance ranking table.

## Recommendation Patterns

- Prioritize expense categories with both high share and fast growth.
- Investigate low-margin business lines before scaling revenue.
- Watch cash flow even when accounting profit looks healthy.
- Separate temporary timing effects from structural profitability issues.
- Link budget variance to departments and controllable drivers.

## Missing Data To Flag

- Cost data is required for margin.
- Expense data is required for net profit.
- Cash movement data is required for cash flow.
- Budget data is required for budget variance.
- Business unit or category fields are required for structural diagnosis.
