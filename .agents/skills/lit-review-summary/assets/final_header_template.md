## Part A — Framework recap

Three layers: (1) Tier 1 Comprehensive Reference Sheet per paper (Bibliographic Anchor; Core Argument; Methodology; Evidence & Findings; Critical Analysis; Synthesis Links; Quotable Lines); (2) Tier 2 Cheat Sheet (citation, thesis, method, finding, gap, connection); (3) Tier 3 Synthesis Matrix (themes × studies with a "what we know" column), organized by theme, not chronology. The purpose of a literature review is to find the empty space the new research fills. Part C is where that space is mapped.
