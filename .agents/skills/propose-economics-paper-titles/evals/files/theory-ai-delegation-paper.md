# Theory paper materials: AI Delegation and Worker Initiative

> **Note:** This is a synthetic teaching and testing packet. All names, data, results, and citations are fictional.

The paper develops a two-period model of managerial delegation, costly worker information acquisition, and an AI recommendation of known precision. Delegation and total surplus are hump-shaped in AI precision when worker information is costly and workers capture some surplus. Non-monotonicity disappears under specific parameter conditions. A complementary-AI extension produces monotonic delegation. The paper distinguishes substitutive and complementary AI and studies worker rents. It has no data, estimation, calibrated welfare calculation, or policy experiment.
