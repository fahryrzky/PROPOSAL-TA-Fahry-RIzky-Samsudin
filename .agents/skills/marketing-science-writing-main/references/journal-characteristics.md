# Marketing Journal Characteristics

## Marketing Science (MktSci)

**Publisher**: INFORMS | **Typical Length**: 35 pages | **Review Rounds**: 2-3

**Editorial Philosophy**: Marketing Science publishes quantitative, model-driven research on marketing phenomena. The journal values **substantive marketing problems** addressed with **rigorous quantitative methods**. Papers should combine a formal model with empirical analysis; pure theory or pure empirics are rarer.

**What MktSci Values**:
1. Substantive marketing problem (not just a methodological exercise)
2. Structural model that captures key trade-offs
3. Novel data that enables identification of model parameters
4. Counterfactual analysis showing managerial implications
5. Generalizable insights beyond the specific empirical context

**Methodology**: Structural econometrics (BLP, dynamic structural models), game-theoretic models, field experiments with structural interpretation, machine learning + causal inference.

**Paper Structure**: Introduction (motivating example) → Model → Data → Identification → Estimation → Counterfactuals → Implications

---

## Journal of Marketing Research (JMR)

**Publisher**: AMA | **Typical Length**: 40 pages | **Review Rounds**: 2-3

**JMR Values**: Methodological rigor in service of substantive marketing problems. Strong emphasis on **identification credibility** and **managerial relevance**. Welcomes experiments, quasi-experiments, and structural models.

**Methodology**: Field experiments (A/B tests), lab experiments, quasi-experiments (DID, RDD, IV), structural models, psychometrics, machine learning.

**Key Features**: "Implications for marketing practice" is a required section. Pre-registration is encouraged for experiments. Replication studies are welcome.

---

## Journal of Marketing (JM)

**Publisher**: AMA | **Typical Length**: 40 pages | **Review Rounds**: 2-3

**JM Values**: Broad conceptual contributions with multi-method empirical validation. Strategy-oriented. The journal's mission is to develop and disseminate knowledge about **real-world marketing questions** useful to scholars, educators, managers, and policymakers. Strong emphasis on conceptual frameworks and theoretical contribution.

**Methodology**: Multi-method (survey + secondary data + experiments), meta-analysis, conceptual frameworks, qualitative + quantitative.

---

## Journal of Consumer Research (JCR)

**Publisher**: Oxford | **Typical Length**: 40 pages | **Review Rounds**: 2-3

**JCR Values**: Consumer behavior at the individual or group level. Psychological mechanisms. Theory-driven. Experimental methods dominate.

**Methodology**: Lab experiments (primary), field experiments, surveys, qualitative (ethnography, interviews), meta-analysis. Consumer psychology theory: construal level, regulatory focus, identity, affect, social influence, etc.

---

## JAMS / IJRM / QME / Marketing Letters

| Journal | Key Differentiator |
|---------|-------------------|
| **JAMS** | Broader scope; conceptual + empirical; strong on review papers and meta-analyses; AMA-affiliated |
| **IJRM** | European tradition; more open to diverse methods; strong special issue culture |
| **QME** | Structural IO approach; rigorous econometrics; smaller volume, higher technical bar |
| **Marketing Letters** | Short format (~20 pages); sharp empirical contributions; replication-friendly |
